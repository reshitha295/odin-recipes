CHURUMURI

Churumuri (also known as Mandakki Upkari or Puffed Rice Chat) is a crunchy, vibrant street-style snack popular in South India, especially Karnataka. It features crispy puffed rice tossed with fresh grated vegetables, roasted peanuts, fragrant spices, and a generous splash of raw, pungent mustard oil or coconut oil for its signature spicy kick.

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups puffed rice (mandakki / muri, lightly dry-roasted if not crispy)
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 small tomato (finely chopped, seeds removed)
  • ¼ cup carrot (finely grated)
  • ¼ cup roasted peanuts
  • 2 tbsp fresh coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp raw coconut oil or mustard oil (essential for street flavor)
  • ½ tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • ¼ tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ cup sev or karaboondi (for final crunch)

Steps:

  1. Prep the Flavor Base: In a large mixing bowl, add the chopped onion, tomato, grated carrot, roasted peanuts, red chili powder, chaat masala, salt, lemon juice, and oil. Mix these ingredients together thoroughly so the spices infuse into the vegetables.
  2. Add the Crunch: Dump the fresh, crispy puffed rice directly into the flavor mixture.
  3. Toss Quickly: Working rapidly with a spoon or your hands, toss everything together until the puffed rice is evenly coated with the spices and oil. Do not overmix, or the puffed rice will turn soggy.
  4. Garnish & Serve: Immediately transfer the mixture into a bowl or a paper cone. Top generously with fine sev, karaboondi, and fresh coriander.
